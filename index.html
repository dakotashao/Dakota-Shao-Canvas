Questions
What kind of designer are you?

I like stepping into unfamiliar systems, figuring out what actually matters, and choosing the right approach from there.

What do you care about most in a project?

Clarity. I like turning something messy, technical, or overwhelming into something people can actually understand and use.

What kinds of projects excite you?

Projects where there’s a real system to unpack—healthcare, mobility, AI, public experiences, or anything that goes beyond just making another app.

What are you like to work with?

Curious, direct, and pretty involved. I ask a lot of questions, catch small inconsistencies, and usually keep pushing until the logic feels right.

Do you code?

Enough to prototype ideas, understand what’s feasible, and have much better conversations with engineers.

What do you do outside of design?

I dance, play guitar, recently started learning drums, and I’m usually picking up something new just because I want to know how it works.

What’s something people don’t expect about you?

I can be very systems-oriented in my work, but I’m also drawn to playful, weird, and slightly unexpected interactions.
